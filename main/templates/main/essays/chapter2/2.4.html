The Interaction of Supply and Demand

Unit 2.4 in Chapter 2: The price system and the microeconomy

WITH THE HELP OF A DIAGRAM(S), EXPLAIN WHAT IS MEANT BY EQUILIBRIUM IN A MARKET, AND CONSIDER THE EXTENT TO WHICH THE EQUILIBRIUM PRICE AND EQUILIBRIUM QUANTITY ARE LIKELY TO CHANGE FOR A PRODUCT FOLLOWING AN INCREASE IN THE WAGES FOR LABOUR ACROSS THE WHOLE ECONOMY.

SOURCE: CAIE, ECONOMICS, 9708, PAPER 2, VARIANT 2, MAY / JUNE, 2023, QUESTION 3A, 8 MARKS

Model Answer written by Choo Pei Qi:

AO1 (3 MARKS)

The equilibrium point is where the quantity demanded equals the quantity supplied and there is no tendency to change in a market.

AO2 (3 MARKS)

With an increase in wage costs, cost of production increases. Hence, the supply curve shifts inwards (SS1→SS2) and the market equilibrium changes (E1→E2). The equilibrium price increases (P1→ P2) while equilibrium quantity decreases (Q1→Q2). However, the extent of the change in equilibrium price and quantity is dependent on the price elasticity of supply. A more elastic supply curve would result in smaller changes in equilibrium price and quantity as shown in diagram 2 and vise versa as shown in diagram 1.

AO3 (2 MARKS)

To conclude, an increase in wages typically leads to a decrease in supply, causing the equilibrium price to rise and the equilibrium quantity to fall. The extent of these changes depend on the elasticity of supply.
