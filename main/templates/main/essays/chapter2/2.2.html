Price Elasticity, Income Elasticity and Cross Elasticity of Demand

Unit 2.2 in Chapter 2: The price system and the microeconomy

ASSESS WHETHER PRICE ELASTICITY OF DEMAND (PED) OR INCOME ELASTICITY OF DEMAND (YED) IS LIKELY TO BE OF GREATER IMPORTANCE TO A FIRM PRODUCING CARS.

SOURCE: CAIE, ECONOMICS, 9708, PAPER 2, VARIANT 1, OCTOBER / NOVEMBER, 2023, QUESTION 2B, 12 MARKS

VIDEO REFERENCE LINK

Model Answer written by Choo Pei Qi:

AO1 (2 MARKS)

Price elasticity of demand (PED) is a measure of responsiveness of quantity demanded to changes in price of the product. It is the ratio of percentage change in quantity demanded to percentage change of price. On the other hand, income elasticity of demand (YED) measures the responsiveness of quantity demanded of a product to a change in consumer income. It is the ratio of percentage change in quantity demanded to percentage change in income.

AO2 (6 MARKS)

PED is useful for the firm for pricing strategies. If the demand for cars is price elastic (magnitude of PED>1), a small change in price leads to a proportionately larger change in quantity demanded. This implies that lowering prices can significantly boost sales volume which can be beneficial during promotional campaigns or in highly competitive markets. Conversely, if demand is price inelastic (magnitude of PED<1), changes in prices will not significantly affect sales volume. This allows the firm to increase price to boost total revenue without losing many customers. Understanding PED helps in setting optimal pricing strategies to maximise revenue. For example, luxury car brands often face inelastic demand, allowing them to maintain high prices and high profit margins.

YED indicates how demand changes with economic conditions. For cars, YED is typically positive and greater than one (income elastic), especially for luxury cars. This means that demand for cars increases at a proportionately larger rate than an increase in income. Understanding YED helps firms in product planning and diversification. For instance, during economic growth, firms can introduce more high-end models, while in recessionary periods, they could focus on more affordable, fuel-efficient models.

AO3 (4 MARKS)

However, the extent to which both may be useful in reality depends on the accuracy of the estimate of PED and YED. It can be difficult for firms to obtain accurate data and therefore obtain accurate PED and YED estimates. It could be argued that PED values are more useful because it allows the firms to make proactive decisions regarding their pricing strategies. However, a firm will need to take into account the availability of substitutes, the amount spent on the product and the time period under consideration. On the other hand, YED will be helpful in giving a firm an indication of the extent to which a change in income may affect spending on cars. However, a firm will need to account for whether a car is seen as a normal or inferior good, the proportion of income spent on the car and the economic environment.

To conclude, both PED and YED are vital for a firm producing cars in theory but their relative importance can vary based on specific market conditions and strategic objectives. Firms must balance their focus on both elasticities to optimise pricing and market strategies effectively.
