Reasons for Government Intervention in Markets

Unit 3.1 in Chapter 3: Government microeconomic intervention

VERY HIGH CONSUMPTION OF CHOCOLATE CAN LEAD TO HEALTH PROBLEMS SUCH AS OBESITY AND DIABETES, ESPECIALLY AMONGST YOUNG CHILDREN IN DEVELOPED ECONOMIES. DISCUSS WHAT IS LIKELY TO BE THE MOST EFFECTIVE POLICY TO REDUCE THESE PROBLEMS.

SOURCE: CAIE, ECONOMICS, 9708, PAPER 2, VARIANT 3, OCTOBER / NOVEMBER, 2019, QUESTION 2B, 12 MARKS

Model Answer written by Thura:

As high chocolate consumption is not ideal for young children, it is important for the governments to intervene in the chocolate market to reduce overconsumption.

AO2 (4 MARKS)
Explanation of 1 method (indirect taxation)

Indirect taxation is one of the most suitable methods to reduce consumption. The government can raise indirect taxes on chocolate, leading to higher sales prices of chocolate. Thus, it will lower the demand. Likewise, the state can also reduce the indirect taxes on the substitutes of chocolate, such as sugar-free sweets, which will lower their prices, thereby switching the consumer demand to substitutes instead of chocolate. Thus, indirect taxation is said to be an effective method to reduce undesirable diseases like obesity and diabetes from overconsuming chocolates.

However, chocolate has an addictive nature. Thus, an increase in price due to indirect prices will not substantially lower its consumption. The demand for chocolate may be price inelastic, thus not reducing the health problems posed by chocolate consumption. Moreover, the price of chocolate occupies a tiny portion of an average person's income. Therefore, even if the price may be higher than usual, he/she will not be very responsive to price, thereby having fewer effects on demand.

AO2 (4 MARKS)
Explanation of another further method (information gap)

Sometimes, chocolate overconsumption can be attributed to information failure (young children may not understand the negative consequences of excessive consumption of chocolates). To fill this information gap, the government can use the provision of information techniques. This will allow young children to understand the health problems of overeating chocolates. Thus, they'll avoid overconsuming it, thereby lowering the health risk of obesity and diabetes.

However, information provision requires a heavy load of effort and funds by the government. Thus, there will be opportunity costs. For example, the government won't be able to provide more subsidies to healthcare services that can directly cure obesity and diabetes, or there will be less investment in education. Moreover, the effectiveness of information provision cannot be measured. Therefore, it is impossible to assess whether the reduction in chocolate consumption is due to the government,s information provision or other factors such as changing consumer preferences.

AO3 (4 MARKS)

Overall, both policies may be effective in reducing health problems such as obesity and diabetes. While indirect taxes can influence chocolate price and demand, the information provision can fill up the information gap, presumably a cause of overconsumption. To conclude, instead of using one specific policy, it is always advisable for a government to apply a combination of policies that can effectively lower the health problems caused by chocolate consumption.
