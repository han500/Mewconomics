Unit 1.3 in Chapter 1: Basic economic ideas and resources

OUTLINE THE FUNCTIONS OF FACTOR ENTERPRISE IN A MODERN ECONOMY AND EXPLAIN HOW ENTERPRISE RESPONDS TO A RISE IN DEMAND FOR A GOOD.

SOURCE: CAIE, ECONOMICS, 9708, PAPER 2, VARIANT 1, MAY / JUNE, 2016, QUESTION 3A, 8 MARKS

Model Answer written by Arissa Hani:

AO1 (2 MARKS)
Knowledge and Understanding of Organisation + Risk-Taking

A modern economy allows market forces such as supply and demand to guide resource allocation in society. Thus, enterprises have the freedom to allocate resources in firms to produce goods and services. An entrepreneur is someone who is always willing to take risks in combining all aspects of production to produce high-quality goods and services at the lowest possible cost in order to maximise profit.

AO2 (3 MARKS)
Explains profit + enterprise in market economy

Therefore, in a free market economy, enterprises will pursue a low-cost method of production, such as adapting capital intensive methods like the use of machinery, cutting down on the cost of wages. Profit is seen as an incentive for entrepreneurs who invest in the three other factors of production (land, labour and capital), as they can use the profit to improve and increase the use of technological innovation. Hence, streamlining the efficiency and productivity of the production of goods and services.

AO3 (3 MARKS)
Analysis application showing how enterprise responds to an increase in demand

As we all know, in a modern economy, competition between firms is very high as they compete to attract consumer spending to purchase their respective products. Therefore, they respond quickly to the price mechanism of a free economy, specifically the law of demand for goods. The law of demand states that as the price of a good decreases, the quantity demanded of a good will increase. Therefore, enterprises will respond to increased demand by increasing methods of production of goods so that it is more efficient to produce a high amount of goods to meet increasing demand from consumers. Moreover, this acts as a sign to entrepreneurs to redirect resources and three factors of production to produce a specific good, as it is the best way to maximise profit and avoid waste.

Not to mention, entrepreneurs will strategise how to increase production of goods and services by hiring more labour-intensive workers. As a result, the country's unemployment rate will rise, raising its living standards.
