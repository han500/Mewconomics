Circular Flow of Income

Unit 4.2 in Chapter 4: The Macroeconomy

WITH THE HELP OF A CIRCULAR FLOW DIAGRAM, EXPLAIN HOW EQUILIBRIUM IS DETERMINED IN AN OPEN ECONOMY AND CONSIDERED WHETER A BUDGET DEFICIT OR A TRADE DEFICIT IS MORE LIKELY TO CAUSE LASTING DISEQUILIBRIUM.

SOURCE: CAIE, ECONOMICS, 9708, PAPER 2, VARIANT 3, MAY / JUNE, 2023, QUESTION __, 8 MARKS

Model Answer written by Anuj:

AO1 (3 MARKS)

An accurate diagram showing income flows between firms and households for reference. The diagram should clearly indicate imports and exports (1); as well as government spending and taxation (1); between firms and households (1).

AO2 (3 MARKS)

Leakages are where money outflows in the form of savings, taxation and imports. Injections are money inflows in the form of investment, government spending and exports. At the heart of the circular flow diagram are households and firms. Households provide factors of production (land, labour, capital, and entrepreneurship) to firms, which produce goods and services. In return, firms then pay households for their inputs in the form of wages, rent, interest, and profit respectively.

Equilibrium is achieved when the sum of injections equals the sum of leakages. If injections exceed leakages, the economy will grow, leading to increased output and income. Conversely, if leakages exceed injections, the economy will contract, leading to reduced output and income.

AO3 (2 MARKS)

A budget deficit occurs when government spending exceeds overall government revenue or taxation. This increases the total injections into the economy, potentially leading to economic growth if the spending stimulates demand and productivity. While both budget and trade deficits can cause disequilibrium, a trade deficit is more likely to cause lasting disequilibrium. This is because it represents a continual outflow of domestic income, weakening the economy’s capacity to generate wealth in the long run.
