    for students, by students

    The Mewconomics website is a student-driven website designed to support Lower Sixth students in their Economics studies. Over the course of six months, a dedicated team of Upper Sixth students wrote original essays for every subtopic in the AS-level syllabus. These essays were meticulously reviewed and edited to ensure clarity and accuracy.

    The website was created with the goal of simplifying complex economic subtopics into accessible, well-organized content. Each essay is structured in the style of an AS Economics essay, incorporating real-life examples, clear definitions and explanations on various economic concepts and easy-to-follow audio explanations. Each section is also divided into A01, A02 and A03 to illustrate how marks are divided and allocated for each section.

    Many thanks goes to Ms. Shir Li for proposing the idea of launching this website and to all the U6 Computer Science and Economics students that were involved in the development of this website!
